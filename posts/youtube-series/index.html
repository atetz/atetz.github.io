Integration sandbox transformed to YouTube series

August 20, 2026

Sometimes a project opportunity lands on my desk for something I haven't tackled before, but I think I can work it out as I go.

Together with Fluxygen, I turned the transport and logistics use case from the integration sandbox I built into a 6-episode YouTube series (in Dutch) on building integrations.

Each episode digs into a different piece of the puzzle:

  • the business case
  • what to check before you start an integration project
  • working with APIs with Postman and the Swagger pages
  • building and testing the integrations
  • how to set up error handling
